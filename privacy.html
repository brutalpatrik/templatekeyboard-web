Privacy Policy - Template Keyboard

Last updated: January 2025

Data Collection

Template Keyboard does not collect, use, or share any personal data.

Local Storage

All templates and variables you create are stored locally on your device using iOS App Groups. This data never leaves your device.

Network

Template Keyboard does not make any network requests. It works completely offline.

Third-party Services

Template Keyboard does not integrate with any third-party services or analytics.

Contact

If you have questions, please contact us at support@templatekeyboard.app
